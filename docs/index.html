Plain Launcher replaces MIUI’s home screen. No App Vault news feed, no “recommended” apps, no ads, and nothing to root or flash.

What goes

  • App Vault’s news feed and promoted cards
  • Ads and “recommended” apps on the home screen and in folders
  • Ads in the installer, cleaner and security scan, with Ad Shield (post #3)

What stays

  • Your step history, from MIUI’s own step counter (post #2)
  • Weather and your real next alarm, under the date
  • Widgets, folders, icon packs and pages

The alarm under the date is your real alarm

MIUI’s own home screen can show an alarm you never set: Xiaomi Calendar registers its midnight widget refresh as an “alarm clock”. Plain Launcher reads the Clock app’s own value instead.

Clock has no alarm, so nothing is shown as one. MIUI’s launcher said “Tue 00:00” here.

One gesture each way

  • Swipe upApp drawer, with search and an A–Z scroller
  • Swipe rightYour Steps page, like App Vault, without the feed
  • Swipe downNotifications, from anywhere on the home screen
  • Double-tapLocks the screen (optional, needs one accessibility switch)

Also in v0.2.0

  • Tap the weather for MIUI Weather, the alarm for Clock, the steps for your Steps page
  • Icon shapes (circle, squircle, rounded square), icon packs, hidden apps, grid sizes
  • Notification dots, a work-profile-aware app list, themed app icon on Android 13+
  • No ads, no analytics, no account. The only network request is the weather (Open-Meteo).

Known issues read before installing
  • Tested hands-on on one phone so far: a Redmi Note 7 (MIUI 12.5, Android 10). Other phones are supported by the code but not yet tested in hand.
  • Recents and the gesture-navigation animations stay MIUI’s. No third-party launcher can change those.
  • Some older MIUI versions don’t allow full-screen gestures with a third-party launcher. Use the 3-button bar there.
  • On phones without MIUI’s step store, the step history starts on the day you turn steps on.
  • Ad Shield’s Realme list is untested, and there is no Samsung list yet.

Your step history, without App Vault

MIUI counts your steps all day in a system service, and App Vault used to show them. Plain Launcher reads the same data, so the numbers match what you saw before.

Swipe right on the first home page to open it.

  • Today against your goal, with distance, active time and calories (distance and calories are estimates)
  • An hourly chart: tap or drag across the bars to see each hour
  • 7-day and 30-day history; tap a day to open it
  • MIUI only keeps about a day of raw steps, so Plain Launcher saves its own history on the phone, every 30 minutes

Count your walks in MyNetDiary and other apps

Turn on Send walks to Health Connect in Home settings. Each walk of 5 minutes or more becomes a Walking workout in Health Connect, and apps like MyNetDiary add the calories from your weight. Nothing to type in by hand.

Not on a Xiaomi phone? Steps come from Health Connect (Samsung Health, Fit, Android 14+), or Plain Launcher records them from the phone’s step sensor in the background.

Ad Shield: switch off MIUI’s ad apps, and switch them back on

A launcher can only clean up the home screen. MIUI’s other ads come from system apps such as msa and analytics. Ad Shield is a script you run from a computer over USB: it disables those apps for your user only. Nothing is uninstalled, core system apps are on a protected list it never touches, and restore undoes exactly what it did.

# on a Linux or macOS computer with adb, phone connected with USB debugging on
git clone https://github.com/tanim913/plain_launcher
cd plain_launcher/adshield
./adshield.sh apply          # dry run: shows what would change
./adshield.sh apply --yes    # do it
./adshield.sh restore --yes  # undo everything

Then turn off the switches only MIUI’s settings can reach: Settings › Passwords & security › Privacy › Ad services › Personalized ad recommendations, and “Receive recommendations” in Security, Themes, File Manager, Downloads and the installer.

Install in four steps

  1. Download the APK

    Get PlainLauncher-0.2.0.apk from GitHub Releases on your phone.

  2. Open it and install

    Tap the file in your browser or File Manager. If Android asks, allow installing from that app. MIUI may show a security scan; tap Install anyway.

  3. Make it your home screen

    Press Home, pick Plain Launcher and choose Always. Or use the banner on the home screen.

  4. Optional: run Ad Shield

    From a computer, to switch off the ads in MIUI’s other system apps (post #3).

Changed your mind? Settings › Apps › Default apps › Home screen › MIUI System Launcher brings MIUI’s home screen back.

Questions root, safety, updates
  • Does it need root? No. It is an ordinary app, set as your home screen.
  • Why not the Play Store? Not yet. Releases are on GitHub, built from the public source.
  • Does it collect anything? No analytics and no account. Steps and settings stay on the phone; the only request it makes is for the weather.
  • How do I update? Download the newer APK from Releases and open it; it installs over the old one and keeps your layout.
